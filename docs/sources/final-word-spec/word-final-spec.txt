Aperture: Final Structure State & Setup Classification Specification

Consolidated project decisions from the independent design challenges

Purpose. This document is the final project-ready specification for the two foundational descriptive engines in Aperture: the Structure State Engine and the Setup Classification Engine. It consolidates the final architectural decisions, formulas, thresholds, lifecycle rules, evidence requirements, interfaces, testing approach, calibration philosophy, and deliberate exclusions.

Core architectural rule. Measurements describe observable market facts. Structure classifies directional condition. Setup classifies a specific price/volatility configuration or event. Lifecycle records where a setup is in its development. Leadership, regime, extension, earnings risk, actionability, position sizing, and portfolio policy remain independent downstream layers.

Final Aperture pipeline:

Market Regime -> Groups -> Leadership -> Structure -> Setup -> Actionability -> Risk -> Portfolio Context

Non-negotiable separation of concerns

Structure must not encode setup type, relative strength, group strength, extension, earnings, regime, or actionability.

Setup must not decide whether a setup should be traded.

Extension is not a structure state.

Reclaim is not a V1 setup family; it is a deterministic evidence/event flag.

Multiple setup families may coexist on one stock.

No arbitrary confidence percentage is produced by either engine.

Every historical classification must be reconstructable from stored numeric evidence and versioned rules.




Part I - Structure State Engine

1. Final state taxonomy

Internal enum	UI label	Conceptual meaning
NEUTRAL	Neutral	No sufficiently established directional structure.
TRANSITION_UP	Emerging	Structure is rotating positively but is not yet a mature uptrend.
TREND_UP	Uptrend	Established constructive intermediate swing structure.
TRANSITION_DOWN	Deteriorating	Material structural damage is developing, but decline is not yet mature.
TREND_DOWN	Decline	Established negative intermediate swing structure.



Why these five states

NEUTRAL replaces BASE because 'base' is a setup concept. A sideways stock may be neutral without being a constructive base.

EMERGING and DETERIORATING are retained because inflection points matter materially to swing traders.

UPTREND and DECLINE are established directional states.

The internal vocabulary is directionally symmetric, which supports future short-side use without doubling the state count.

2. Moving averages and roles

Average	Used in classifier?	Role
EMA10	No	Setup/location/trade-management context; too reactive for structural state authority.
SMA20	Yes	Fast structural trend, roughly one trading month.
SMA50	Yes	Intermediate structural anchor, roughly ten trading weeks.
SMA200	No	Long-term context only; must not delay short-horizon structural transitions.



Key decision:

The classifier is driven by the 20-day/50-day complex, not a 10/20/50/200 stack. EMA10 and SMA200 should be calculated and stored, but they do not vote on the state. This sharply reduces redundant signals and avoids state churn during normal short-term pullbacks.

3. Exact mathematical inputs

Adjusted daily OHLC data.

Wilder ATR14.

SMA20 and SMA50.

ATR-normalized close distance from SMA20 and SMA50.

ATR-normalized SMA20/SMA50 separation.

ATR-normalized SMA20 and SMA50 slope.

Ten-session persistence of closes above SMA20.

Previous state and persistence counters.

4. Core formulas

ATR14 = Wilder ATR(14)

D20 = (Close - SMA20) / ATR14

D50 = (Close - SMA50) / ATR14

Spread2050 = (SMA20 - SMA50) / ATR14

Slope20 = (SMA20[t] - SMA20[t-10]) / median(ATR14[t-9:t])

Slope50 = (SMA50[t] - SMA50[t-20]) / median(ATR14[t-19:t])

P20 = count(Close > SMA20 over t-9:t) / 10

Why ATR normalization? A fixed dollar or percentage threshold behaves very differently in a 2% ADR stock versus an 8% ADR stock. ATR units create a more comparable structural language across volatility profiles. Median ATR is used in slope denominators to reduce distortion from a single volatility shock.

5. State qualification rules

EMERGING entry

E1 D50 >= +0.25

E2 Slope20 >= +0.15

E3 Spread2050 >= -0.25

E4 Slope50 >= -0.10

E5 P20 >= 0.60

Require E1 AND E2 AND at least 4 of 5 conditions for 2 consecutive sessions.

UPTREND entry

U1 D50 >= -0.25

U2 Spread2050 >= +0.25

U3 Slope20 >= +0.25

U4 Slope50 >= +0.15

U5 P20 >= 0.60

Require U1 AND U4 AND at least 4 of 5 conditions for 3 consecutive sessions.

UPTREND retention

H1 D50 >= -0.50

H2 Spread2050 >= 0.00

H3 Slope50 >= +0.05

H4 P20 >= 0.40

Require H1 AND H3 AND at least 3 of 4 conditions.

DETERIORATING from UPTREND

D1 D20 <= -0.50

D2 P20 <= 0.40

D3 Slope20 <= +0.05

D4 Spread2050 <= +0.20

D5 D50 <= +0.50

Require D1 AND D2 AND at least 4 of 5 conditions for 2 consecutive sessions.

DECLINE entry

L1 D50 <= +0.25

L2 Spread2050 <= -0.25

L3 Slope20 <= -0.25

L4 Slope50 <= -0.15

L5 P20 <= 0.40

Require L1 AND L4 AND at least 4 of 5 conditions for 3 consecutive sessions.

Short-side symmetry. The downside model should be implemented as the sign-inverted mirror of the upside rules wherever possible. This keeps the architecture consistent and avoids maintaining separate discretionary long and short models.

6. State transitions and hysteresis

Current state	Allowed next states
NEUTRAL	NEUTRAL, EMERGING, DETERIORATING
EMERGING	EMERGING, UPTREND, NEUTRAL, DETERIORATING
UPTREND	UPTREND, DETERIORATING, NEUTRAL
DETERIORATING	DETERIORATING, DECLINE, NEUTRAL, UPTREND
DECLINE	DECLINE, EMERGING, NEUTRAL



Do not normally allow UPTREND -> DECLINE or DECLINE -> UPTREND directly.

Use stricter thresholds to enter a mature trend and looser thresholds to retain it.

Require two sessions for transitional states and three sessions for mature directional states.

If UPTREND retention fails for five consecutive sessions but explicit bearish damage does not fire, transition to NEUTRAL.

Mirror the same logic on the downside.

7. Extraordinary gaps / shock override

Positive shock override:

D50 >= +2.0 AND Close > SMA20 AND Slope20 > -0.10

=> NEUTRAL or DECLINE may move immediately to EMERGING.



Negative shock override:

D50 <= -2.0 AND Close < SMA20 AND Slope20 < +0.10

=> positive/neutral states may move immediately to DETERIORATING.

Important: A violent one-day event can accelerate entry into a transitional state, but it cannot establish a mature trend by itself.

8. Extension is separate

Final decision: EXTENDED is not a structure state. A stock can remain structurally UPTREND while its extension layer says NORMAL, ELEVATED, or EXTREME. This preserves orthogonality between trend condition and trade location.

Example:

Structure = UPTREND

Extension = EXTREME

9. Constructive pullbacks and bases

A normal pullback to EMA10/SMA20/SMA50 should remain UPTREND while the Setup Engine detects TREND_PULLBACK.

A six-week consolidation above a rising SMA50 may remain UPTREND while the Setup Engine detects RANGE and/or CONTRACTION.

A directionless months-long sideways stock is NEUTRAL, even if the Setup Engine separately identifies a RANGE.

Structure never decides whether a range is a 'good base.'

10. Structure evidence and state history

Store at minimum:

symbol, session, current_state, previous_state, state_entered_date, sessions_in_state,

close, sma20, sma50, atr14, d20_atr, d50_atr, spread20_50_atr,

slope20_10_atr, slope50_20_atr, p20,

all state predicate booleans, conditions_passed, threshold margins,

qualification-day counters, retention status, shock_override, transition_reason,

engine_version, feature_version, threshold_version, data/adjustment revision.

Store previous-state duration and last transition date/reason.

Track transition_count_20d and transition_count_60d as diagnostics, not classification features.

Expose passed and failed conditions rather than an arbitrary confidence percentage.

11. Deliberately excluded from Structure V1

RSI, MACD, ADX/DMI, Bollinger Bands.

Volume and relative strength.

Group strength and market regime.

Earnings or event risk.

52-week-high requirements.

Higher-high/higher-low pivot algorithms.

Linear-regression slope.

EMA10 and SMA200 as voting inputs.

Extension and compression.

Machine learning or LLM chart judgment.




Part II - Setup Classification Engine

12. Final V1 setup taxonomy

Setup type	What it represents	What it does not decide
EP	Unusually large directional information gap with strong participation and close.	Catalyst identity, quality, leadership, actionability.
CONTRACTION	Progressive compression in closing dispersion and realized range.	Textbook VCP artistry or pivot-leg interpretation.
TREND_PULLBACK	Return toward a trend reference after prior excursion while external structure remains intact.	Whether the pullback should be bought.
RANGE	Sustained bounded and relatively horizontal trading area with objective upper/lower boundaries.	Cup/handle, double bottom, saucer, base count.



Reclaim decision. RECLAIM is not a fifth V1 setup. It is a deterministic event/evidence flag that can annotate Pullback, Emerging, Range, or EP.

13. Multiple simultaneous setups

Use active_setups[] rather than primary_setup/secondary_setup.

Do not rank setup families. Priority belongs downstream in policy/actionability.

A stock may simultaneously be RANGE + CONTRACTION, or EP + RANGE_BREAKOUT.

Prevent duplicate instances with one active instance per setup_type + direction + reference.

14. Universal setup lifecycle

FORMING -> NEAR_TRIGGER -> TRIGGERED -> RESOLVED

Any active state -> FAILED

Any pre-trigger state -> STALE

Lifecycle	Definition
FORMING	Setup conditions exist but price is not yet sufficiently close to its trigger/reference.
NEAR_TRIGGER	Setup remains valid and price is within the configured ATR distance of the trigger.
TRIGGERED	The objective trigger boundary or contact condition has occurred; this does not mean BUY.
RESOLVED	Post-trigger observation window ended without satisfying failure; this does not mean profitable.
FAILED	Explicit setup invalidation occurred.
STALE	Pre-trigger setup aged out or geometry drifted enough to become a new setup.



Critical lifecycle rule. Lifecycle is monotonic. Once a setup is TRIGGERED it never goes backward to NEAR_TRIGGER. If the setup later reforms, create a new immutable setup_id.

15. Common setup object

SetupInstance {

  setup_id, symbol, session, setup_type, direction, lifecycle,

  first_detected_date, lifecycle_entered_date, age_sessions,

  reference_type, reference_level, distance_to_reference_atr,

  trigger_level, invalidation_level,

  structure_state_ref, transition_reason,

  feature_version, setup_engine_version, threshold_version, data_version

}

16. EP / Episodic Information Gap

Measurements

GapPct = (Open[t] - Close[t-1]) / Close[t-1]

GapATR = (Open[t] - Close[t-1]) / ATR14[t-1]

ShockATR = (Close[t] - Close[t-1]) / ATR14[t-1]

MedianVol20_prior = median(Volume[t-20:t-1])

RVOL20 = Volume[t] / MedianVol20_prior

CLV = (Close - Low) / (High - Low)

If High == Low, define CLV = 0.5.

Long EP initial thresholds

GapPct >= +4%

GapATR >= +1.0

ShockATR >= +1.5

RVOL20 >= 1.5

CLV >= 0.65

All five conditions required.

Do not require Close > Open.

Do not require a new recent high.

Use both GapPct and GapATR so the move is large in absolute and stock-specific volatility terms.

EP context may be stored as TRANSITION or CONTINUATION based on Structure, but not as separate setup types.

Bearish EP symmetry

GapPct <= -4%

GapATR <= -1.0

ShockATR <= -1.5

RVOL20 >= 1.5

CLV <= 0.35

setup_type = EP, direction = SHORT

EP lifecycle

EP begins directly at TRIGGERED on the event session.

Store event high, event low, event midpoint, event close, and prior-close gap reference.

Long EP FAILED if Close < event low within the next 5 sessions.

Short EP mirrors the rule.

If no failure after 5 complete sessions, mark RESOLVED.

17. CONTRACTION

Core formulas

CRn = (max(Close over n sessions) - min(Close over n sessions)) / Close[t]

Compute CR20, CR10, CR5

R10_20 = CR10 / CR20

R5_10 = CR5 / CR10

TR5 = mean(TrueRange[t-4:t])

TR20 = mean(TrueRange[t-19:t])

TRCompression = TR5 / TR20

Core definition

C1 CR10 / CR20 <= 0.75

C2 CR5 / CR10 <= 0.75

C3 TR5 / TR20 <= 0.80

C4 CR20 > 0

Require all core conditions.

Volume dry-up

VolumeDryRatio = MedianVolume5 / MedianVolume20

Supporting evidence only: volume_dryup = VolumeDryRatio <= 0.80. It does not veto contraction.

Objective contraction reference

Upper10 = second-highest High over last 10 sessions

Lower10 = second-lowest Low over last 10 sessions

Breakout trigger must use yesterday's frozen Upper10/Lower10, never today's dynamically updated boundary.

Lifecycle

FORMING: core contraction passes and DistancePivotATR > 0.50

NEAR_TRIGGER LONG: 0 < (Upper10 - Close)/ATR14 <= 0.50

TRIGGERED LONG: Close[t] > Upper10[t-1] + 0.10 * ATR14[t-1]

FAILED pre-trigger: TR5/TR20 > 1.10 OR Close < Lower10 - 0.25 ATR

FAILED post-trigger within 5 sessions: Close < TriggerLevel - 0.50 * ATR_at_trigger

RESOLVED: 5 sessions after trigger without failure

STALE: active > 15 sessions without triggering

VCP decision. Do not detect sequential VCP legs in V1. That would require a hidden pivot/swing algorithm and would reintroduce subjective chart artistry.

18. TREND_PULLBACK

One family, three possible references: EMA10, SMA20, SMA50.

Structural compatibility

Long pullback requires structure == UPTREND

Short pullback requires structure == DECLINE

Do not duplicate structure slopes or MA-order rules inside the setup engine.

Distance and approach formulas

DistanceMA_ATR = (Close - MA) / ATR14

Long approach condition: max(DistanceMA_ATR over prior 5 sessions) >= +1.0

Short approach condition: min(DistanceMA_ATR over prior 5 sessions) <= -1.0

Long pullback lifecycle bands

FORMING: +0.75 < DistanceMA_ATR <= +1.50

NEAR_TRIGGER: +0.25 < DistanceMA_ATR <= +0.75

TRIGGERED: -0.50 <= DistanceMA_ATR <= +0.25

If multiple MAs qualify, choose the nearest MA as reference_ma and store all qualifying candidate_references.

Pullback quality metrics such as volume contraction, TR contraction, down-day count, CLV, distance from recent high, and reclaim flags are supporting evidence only.

A normal undercut can still be a valid pullback; that is why the trigger zone extends to -0.50 ATR.

Failure / resolution / staleness

FAILED if DistanceMA_ATR < -0.75 for 2 consecutive sessions

OR immediately if structure becomes DETERIORATING or DECLINE.

RESOLVED after trigger if DistanceMA_ATR >= +1.0 before failure.

STALE if pre-trigger instance lasts >10 sessions without entering trigger zone

OR price moves back above +1.50 ATR before ever triggering.

19. RANGE / BASE

Canonical V1 window: 20 sessions. Do not scan multiple overlapping 15/20/30/40/60-day range families.

Robust boundaries

Upper20 = second-highest High over last 20 sessions

Lower20 = second-lowest Low over last 20 sessions

Range geometry

RangeDepthPct = (Upper20 - Lower20) / ((Upper20 + Lower20)/2)

RangeDepthATR = (Upper20 - Lower20) / median(ATR14 over last 20 sessions)

Efficiency20 = abs(Close[t] - Close[t-19]) / (Upper20 - Lower20)

SMA20Drift = abs(SMA20[t] - SMA20[t-10]) / median(ATR14[t-9:t])

RANGE definition

RangeDepthATR <= 6.0

RangeDepthPct <= 25%

Efficiency20 <= 0.50

SMA20Drift <= 0.50

Require all conditions.

Lifecycle

FORMING: range qualifies and price is >0.50 ATR from both boundaries

NEAR_TRIGGER LONG: 0 < (Upper20 - Close)/ATR14 <= 0.50

NEAR_TRIGGER SHORT: 0 < (Close - Lower20)/ATR14 <= 0.50

TRIGGERED LONG: Close[t] > Upper20[t-1] + 0.10 * ATR14[t-1]

TRIGGERED SHORT: Close[t] < Lower20[t-1] - 0.10 * ATR14[t-1]

FAILED LONG within 3 sessions: Close < TriggerUpper - 0.25 * ATR_at_trigger

FAILED SHORT: symmetric

RESOLVED: 5 sessions after trigger without failure

STALE: pre-trigger age >60 sessions OR boundaries shift by >1 ATR from setup inception

Do not require breakout volume; record it as evidence.

Use closing confirmation rather than intraday high/low alone.

Second-highest/second-lowest boundaries reduce one-wick distortion while remaining fully deterministic.

20. Reclaim event flags

Example SMA20 reclaim flag:

Close[t-1] < SMA20[t-1] AND Close[t] > SMA20[t]

Optional strengthening evidence: Low[t] < SMA20[t] AND CLV[t] > 0.60

Use as context/evidence, not as setup_type. The same pattern applies to EMA10 and SMA50.

22. Relationship with other Aperture layers

Layer	Setup engine behavior
Leadership / RS	Ignore entirely. Low-RS stocks can objectively form setups.
Group strength	Ignore entirely.

23. No setup confidence score

Do not emit 'VCP confidence 82%' or similar.

Expose passed rules, failed rules, numerical threshold margins, lifecycle, age, and contradictions.

A probability should only exist later if it is explicitly calibrated to a defined future event; it should not be disguised as setup classification.

Part III - Integrated Aperture Design

26. Final descriptive grammar

The key outcome of the design work is not just the formulas; it is the separation of descriptive dimensions. Aperture should be able to describe one stock with several independent facts without forcing them into one composite label.

Example 1:

Structure     UPTREND

Setup         TREND_PULLBACK / TRIGGERED

Reference     SMA20

Extension     NORMAL

RS            94

Group         LEADING

Regime        GREEN

Earnings      +18 sessions

Action        ACT



Example 2:

Structure     EMERGING

Setup         EP / TRIGGERED

EP Context    TRANSITION

Extension     EXTREME

RS            91

Regime        YELLOW

Action        WATCH



Example 3:

Structure     UPTREND

Setups        RANGE / NEAR_TRIGGER; CONTRACTION / NEAR_TRIGGER

Extension     NORMAL

Action        determined downstream

27. Hard definitions vs configurable parameters

Hard definitions: feature equations, state/setup semantics, lifecycle semantics, use of frozen prior-session trigger references, separation of layers, monotonic setup lifecycle, versioned historical evidence.

Configurable V1 parameters: ATR buffers, persistence-day counts, EP thresholds, contraction ratios, pullback zones, range depth limits, breakout buffers, failure windows, and staleness windows. These must live in explicit versioned parameter files rather than being scattered through code.

28. Historical reproducibility requirements

Persist daily state/setup snapshots rather than reconstructing only from current code.

Store raw-data timestamp/source and adjustment/corporate-action revision where available.

Version feature formulas, state engine, setup engine, and threshold sets independently.

Store previous state and persistence counters because Structure is path-dependent.

Store frozen reference levels used for setup triggers so later rolling-window changes do not alter history.

Keep policy/action versions outside Structure/Setup evidence.

29. Testing strategy

Structure synthetic fixtures

Flat base -> NEUTRAL.

Gradual emergence -> NEUTRAL -> EMERGING -> UPTREND.

Clean sustained advance -> UPTREND retained.

Normal pullback / brief SMA20 undercut -> remain UPTREND.

Extended advance -> remain UPTREND; extension changes externally.

Slow flattening -> UPTREND -> NEUTRAL.

Genuine rollover -> UPTREND -> DETERIORATING -> DECLINE.

False breakdown and reclaim -> state retained or rapid recovery according to hysteresis.

Large positive gap -> immediate EMERGING, not immediate UPTREND.

Crash gap -> immediate DETERIORATING, not immediate DECLINE.

Noisy SMA20/SMA50 oscillation -> minimal state flipping.

Setup synthetic fixtures

EP: ordinary gap; valid long EP; huge gap with weak close; huge gap without volume; valid bearish EP; fail below event low; survive to RESOLVED.

CONTRACTION: progressive 20->10->5 compression; range compression with TR expansion; one-day tightness; re-expansion failure; near-trigger; breakout; resolved breakout.

TREND_PULLBACK: EMA10/SMA20/SMA50 approaches; stock living under MA; trigger-zone touch; structure deterioration; rebound to RESOLVED.

RANGE: clean horizontal range; directional trend rejected by efficiency; anomalous wick handled by trimmed extrema; near trigger; breakout; failed breakout; breakdown.

Boundary tests

Every major threshold must be tested at threshold-epsilon, threshold, and threshold+epsilon. Also test missing bars, newly listed stocks, zero volume, High=Low, bad OHLC relationships, duplicate sessions, splits, trading halts, null ATR, and corporate-action revisions.

30. Calibration philosophy

Start with the explicit V1 priors in this document; do not optimize immediately.

Inspect broad point-in-time distributions of every core measurement across years, sectors, and volatility regimes.

Randomly inspect positives, near misses, and negatives; do not calibrate only on famous winners.

Include failed setups and failed leaders to avoid hindsight bias.

Measure state duration, setup frequency, stale rate, failure rate, overlap, transition churn, and one-day reversals.

Use walk-forward validation across bull, bear, crisis, quiet, and speculative regimes.

Prefer round, explainable thresholds over numerically optimized values.

Use future returns only after semantics stabilize and only as diagnostics, not as the definition of the setup or state.

31. Deliberately excluded from Setup V1

Cup-and-handle, double bottom, saucer, ascending triangle, wedge, head-and-shoulders.

Flags/pennants as independent families; these are usually contractions inside trends.

Textbook sequential VCP leg recognition.

Pocket pivots.

Reclaim as its own setup family.

Inside day / NR7 as foundational setup types.

Bollinger squeeze and RSI/MACD-based setup definitions.

Fibonacci retracements.

Candlestick-pattern families.

RS, group strength, regime, earnings lockout, extension veto.

LLM or visual chart interpretation.

33. Strongest final recommendation

Ship this as V1 without adding more indicators, states, or chart-pattern families. The strength of the design is that each engine makes a narrow, auditable claim. The Structure Engine answers whether price is neutral, transitioning, or in an established trend. The Setup Engine answers whether one of four measurable configurations or events exists and where it is in its lifecycle. Everything that answers 'should I care?' or 'should I trade it?' remains downstream.

Recommended V1 Structure:

NEUTRAL / EMERGING / UPTREND / DETERIORATING / DECLINE



Recommended V1 Setups:

EP / CONTRACTION / TREND_PULLBACK / RANGE



Recommended lifecycle:

FORMING / NEAR_TRIGGER / TRIGGERED / RESOLVED / FAILED / STALE



Recommended philosophy:

Simple measurable market behavior > mathematical imitation of subjective chart artistry.

Implementation handoff note

Before coding, convert every configurable parameter above into versioned configuration constants and convert every state/setup predicate into pure deterministic functions with unit tests. Persist evidence and lifecycle/state transitions as first-class data, not just UI labels. That will allow Aperture to audit historical decisions and safely evolve thresholds later without rewriting history.
